\section{Numerische Details}

\subsection{Die Simulation}

Die Simulation verwendet:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \( N = 4096 \) Knoten
    \item \( 500 \) Iterationen
    \item GPU-Beschleunigung (OpenCL)
    \item Leapfrog-Integrator
\end{itemize}

\begin{align*}
    \text{SCALE} &= 0.70710678 \\

    \text{RHO\_0} &= 1e-6 \\
    \text{RHO\_MIN} &= 1e-8
\end{align*}
